.. _integrator:

===============
For integrators
===============

Who may see what, how links are checked in the background, how to get the
results by mail and how to keep them accurate.

Good to know before you start:

*   Only the live workspace is checked.
*   Only broken links and notices are stored. A link that is fine leaves no
    row.
*   There is no Page TSconfig for the checks. The fields come from the TCA,
    :ref:`excluded fields <configuration>` are the exception.
